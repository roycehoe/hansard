# Dropped approaches

What I tried on the site and then dropped, newest first, with the reason and the record of the decision. A decision with no public record lives in my private notes.

| Dropped | What I tried | Why it went | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 November 2026 | The hand check's count of wrong credits on the methods page | Without its sample size, its one wrong credit read as the archive's only mistake. The page now gives the error rates alone. | [ADR 0009](adr/0009-ai-readers-person-judges-flags.md), note of 15 November |
| 13 November 2026 | A help page on finding the debates behind a section of an Act | The design should make that plain without a page of instructions. | Not public |
| 13 November 2026 | A page for each Bill | I ruled for it on 12 November and reversed it the next day, keeping the Act page's entries, which already show each Bill with its debates. | Not public |
| 12 November 2026 | Report pages drawn only in the browser | AI tools that read web pages, such as ChatGPT's, run no JavaScript, so they found an empty page. The server now writes each report's text into the page too. | [ADR 0008](adr/0008-pages-render-on-the-client.md), amendment of 12 November |
| 11 November 2026 | A sky-blue focus ring | It was too bright and bold. Focus now shows as a thin gold outline. | [ADR 0005](adr/0005-palette-from-the-chamber.md), second amendment of 11 November |
| 8 and 11 November 2026 | Gold lace, then sky blue, for links | The gold sat too close in hue to the browns around it. I found the blue ugly among the browns. Links went back to the mahogany. | [ADR 0005](adr/0005-palette-from-the-chamber.md), amendments of 8 and 11 November |
| 9 November 2026 | Act links made by a title similarity score of 0.85 or more | Nobody can say why 0.85 passes a pair and 0.84 fails it, while a misprint on a hand-kept list can be read and argued with. Every new link comes from a fixed rule, and the 8 links made by score stay until they move to that list. | [ADR 0011](adr/0011-links-come-from-fixed-rules.md) |
| 7 November 2026 | Fixing the one search a reader reported, `Constitution 1984` | One-off fixes would turn into a stream of hints, so I set a rule for when search may add a note. | [ADR 0010](adr/0010-search-notes.md) |
| 2 November 2026 | Two AI readers, one Sonnet and one Opus, on each hand check | Both caught all 30 planted errors in the first check. Later checks use one Sonnet reader, whose rulebook carries my rulings from the first. | [ADR 0009](adr/0009-ai-readers-person-judges-flags.md), note of 2 November |
| 1 November 2026 | A first hand check, a review page of 50 paragraphs drawn through the site's search | It left eight Parliaments with no paragraphs and skipped written answers, and an agent saved all 50 marks within one second, so no person read them. A fresh draw of 1,000 paragraphs from the database replaced it. | [ADR 0009](adr/0009-ai-readers-person-judges-flags.md) |
