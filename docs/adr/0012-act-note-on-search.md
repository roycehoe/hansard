# 0012. Search may name an Act the words contain

On 12 November 2026 a law graduate said Open Hansard was not good enough for legal research, because typing a statute's name did not lead to the debates on it. Lawyers read those debates to apply the purposive interpretation test. `"Employment Act"` returned over 1,000 passing mentions, led by written answers from 2000, 2005 and 2024, and nothing connected the Act to the Bills that amended it. The law-links data already holds that chain, and the Act page shows it, but a reader who types into search never reaches that page. ADR 0010 does not allow a note for this. A note there may read only tables the page already loads, so it can't check the words against the 523 Act titles without an extra request, and gate G7 holds a note to one subsystem with no extra request. Royce ruled on 12 November 2026 to add a third note alongside the year note and the name note. The Act note shows when the typed words contain a stored Act title, matched exactly after ADR 0011's fixed clean-up, with or without the year the title carries ("Employment Act" matches "Employment Act 1968"). It links to that Act's page and leaves the results alone. It passes every ADR 0010 gate except G4 and G7. The page loads the Act titles with one cacheable request, and the note needs an API list of Acts as well as the frontend. ADR 0010's ban on acronym expansion still holds, so "POHA" shows no note. The cost is one extra request on the search page and a note that misses an Act typed under a short name the title doesn't contain.

## Amendment, 12 Nov 2026

The Act note waits for the public release of the Act pages. Later on 12 November 2026 Royce ruled that the Act pages stay hidden while the law graduate tests them: "we do not display the link anywhere on our webpage. This will be a hidden page." They are released once he gives his go-ahead. Until then no page links to an Act page, the search page included, so the note is not built.

## Amendment, 14 Nov 2026

Royce released the Act pages on 14 November 2026, before the law graduate's go-ahead. The header and phone menus now link the Acts list, the noindex tag is gone from `/acts` and every path under it, and the sitemap lists the Act pages. The Act note on search is still not built and waits on Royce.
