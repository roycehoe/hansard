# 0002. No government identity marks

The site should look like it belongs with modern Singapore Government websites, and its palette comes from Parliament House and national insignia. Copying the identity marks would make it pass for an official site. Those marks are the "A Singapore Government Agency Website" masthead, the State Crest artwork, the Lion Head symbol, gov.sg branding and a gov.sg-style domain. A reader would then trust our parsed and attributed Hansard text as the official record, and it isn't. The Singapore Arms and Flag and National Anthem Act also restricts how the crest and flag can be used. The site borrows layout and interaction patterns from SGDS v3 and Isomer, such as spacing, type scale, navigation, search, tables and footers. It takes colours from insignia, never the artwork itself. The header uses a plain wordmark, and every page footer says the site is unofficial and independent of the Parliament of Singapore. Anyone adding a component or asset that carries an official mark needs to overturn this ADR first.
