# 0013. An Act's Bill links show the date they run to

On 12 November 2026 Royce ruled to show an Act's Bill links with the date they run to, instead of hiding the links of any Act that a later change has left unlinked. The stored Legislative Histories stop in October 2024, and 174 current Acts were changed after that, so hiding would remove the links of at least 133 of the 482 Acts that are linked in full up to then. Every Act's links are complete only as far as the printed record they were read from, and new amending Acts keep opening the gap. Each Act page therefore carries the line "Bills linked up to {date}. Later changes are not linked yet.", with the date written like "14 October 2024".

The date is the as-at date of the printed record the Act's links were read from, which is the date of the SSO copy of that Act. It is not the latest reading date in the Act's history, because an Act copied in December 2024 and one copied in July 2024 can both end on the same amendment. The hand-kept links belong to entries of the same printed history, so they take the same date. An Act with no printed history, and an Act whose copy date is not stored, shows no line. The page does not borrow another Act's date and does not use one date for all Acts.

Nothing is built yet, because the database does not hold that date. On the copy of 9 November 2026, `act` has only `id`, `sso_code` and `title`, and `acthistoryentry` has the printed reading dates and no capture date. The scraper reads each Act from an Internet Archive copy of its SSO PDF taken between July and December 2024, and the copy's timestamp is not kept in the database. The latest second or third reading date printed anywhere is 14 October 2024, but that is the end of the longest history and not the as-at date of any one copy. 27 Acts have no history at all. The line waits for the scraper to store a copy date on each Act, which is a scraper migration and a row in its data-loss register. The eGazette rows are not read by the Act page and production holds none, so the line says nothing about them. If eGazette links later reach an Act page, the date of that Act needs its own ruling.

The note under the Act title, "This history comes from copies of SSO taken between July and December 2024. Later amendments are not listed yet.", stays as it is, and Royce rules on whether the dated line replaces it once the line exists.
